About Level Up Life!

Between lectures, part-time jobs and figuring out where the laundromat even is, most of us pick up “adulting” skills the hard way — by getting something wrong first. This site pulls together eleven short, no-fluff modules on money, safety, time and everything in between, each written by a member of our team. No sign-up, no jargon — just what to do and why it matters.
