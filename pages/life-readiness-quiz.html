How to read your score

  • Every question has 3 answers, worth 1, 2 or 3 points - the more that answer shows you’re already on top of things, the more points it’s worth.
  • You’re asked 4 questions from each of the 4 categories (16 in total), so each category score below is out of 12 points, and your overall score is out of 48.
  • Your overall result is based on what percentage of that 48 you scored: roughly 0–54% is “Just Getting Started”, 55–79% is “Building Momentum”, and 80–100% is “Life Ready”.
  • The advice under “What to try next” points at whichever category you scored lowest in, since that’s usually the easiest place to make a quick improvement - if two or more categories tie for the lowest score, you’ll get advice for each of them.
