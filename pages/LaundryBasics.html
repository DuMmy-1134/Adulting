Home Skills

Laundry without the guesswork.

Master the basics of laundry in simple, easy-to-follow steps. From sorting to drying, we've got you covered.

Step-by-step guide

01

Sort your clothes

Separate into whites, darks, and delicates. Check pockets and close zippers to protect your clothes.

02

Choose the right settings

Use hot water for whites, warm for most items, and cold for colors and delicates to prevent fading.

03

Dry and fold

Hang dry delicate items or use the appropriate dryer heat. Fold immediately to prevent wrinkles.

Do's

  • ✓ Check care labels before washing
  • ✓ Use cold water to save energy
  • ✓ Sort by color to prevent fading
  • ✓ Empty pockets and close zippers
  • ✓ Use appropriate water temperature

Don'ts

  • ✗ Don't overload the washer or dryer
  • ✗ Don't mix colors with whites
  • ✗ Don't leave wet clothes in the washer
  • ✗ Don't use too much detergent
  • ✗ Don't ignore laundry symbols
