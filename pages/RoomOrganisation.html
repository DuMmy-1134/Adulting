Decluttering method: 3-step card experience

01

Assess

Look at each item in your room. Ask yourself: Do I use this? Do I love this? Does it serve a purpose?

02

Decide

Sort items into three piles: keep, donate, or throw away. Be honest about what you actually need and use regularly.

03

Organize

Arrange remaining items in a logical system. Create designated spaces so everything has a home.

Do's

  • ✓ Make a plan before you start organizing
  • ✓ Use vertical space with shelves and wall storage
  • ✓ Invest in good storage containers
  • ✓ Label everything clearly
  • ✓ Maintain your system regularly

Don'ts

  • ✗ Don't keep items out of guilt or "just in case"
  • ✗ Don't buy organizing supplies before decluttering
  • ✗ Don't ignore items that need repairs
  • ✗ Don't forget about high and low spaces
  • ✗ Don't organize in a rushed way
