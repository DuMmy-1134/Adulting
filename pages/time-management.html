          Focused study techniques

          The Pomodoro Technique + –

          Work in focused 25-minute bursts, then take a 5-minute break. After four bursts, take a longer 15–30 minute break. Try it with the timer further down this page.

          Timeboxing + –

          Give every task a fixed block of time on your calendar, even revision or admin. When the box ends, you move on — this stops one task quietly eating your whole afternoon.

          The Two-Minute Rule + –

          If a task takes less than two minutes, do it immediately instead of adding it to a list. It stops tiny tasks from piling up into one huge, overwhelming list.

          Eat the Frog First + –

          Do your least favourite, most-avoided task first thing, while your energy and willpower are highest. Everything after that feels easier by comparison.
