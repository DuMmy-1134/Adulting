Money Basics

Simple steps to build smart financial habits for life.

Needs Vs Wants

Are you able to separate your desires? Is it a need or a want? click to Read →
Need

Groceries, rent, essential clothing, phone plan.

💡 Want

Fast food, designer fits, streaming subs, gaming.

The 3-Question Reality Check

  • 1️⃣ Will my week break down without this item?
  • 2️⃣ Am I buying this out of stress, boredom, or FOMO?
  • 3️⃣ Would I still want this if it wasn't on sale/trending?

Micro-Challenge: Try the 24-Hour Rule

Before buying any 'Want' over $20, leave it in your cart for 24 hours. If you still care about it tomorrow, consider it!

Setting Budget Goals: Interactive Guide

Learn how to save for things you actually want without giving up your lifestyle. Click to Read →

1. Pick Your Savings Buckets

Short-Term 1 – 3 Months

Concert tickets, new shoes, or festival weekend.

Medium-Term 6 – 12 Months

Upgrading tech, laptop, or holiday trip.

Long-Term 1+ Years

First car down payment, moving out fund.

2. The Teen 50/30/20 Split Formula

When you get money (job, allowance, gift), divide it instantly:

50% Needs 30% Fun & Wants 20% Goal Bucket

Pro-Tip: "Out of Sight, Out of Mind"

Create a separate sub-account or savings stash on day 1. If the money stays in your main spending account, you will spend it on small daily purchases without realizing it.

Saving Habits

Set a Small Goal

Imporantance of realistc Goals! Click to Read
🎯 The Rule of Micro-Wins

If a goal takes 6 months, you’ll give up in 2 weeks. Break it into weekly targets you can hit without stress.

Try This Strategy:

Name your savings account. Instead of "Savings", name it "New Laptop Fund". Studies show you are 3x less likely to spend money when it has a label.

⚡ Quick Action Item: Pick a tiny target ($10 this week). Put it away today before you spend it on snacks.
Tracking the Expenses How tracking your Expenses will help you reduce your expenses Click to Read
🕵️ The "Invisible Leak" Effect

A $4 coffee 5 days a week equals $80 a month ($960 a year!). Tracking isn't about quitting coffee—it's about seeing the leaks.

The 3-Bucket Tracking System:
  • 🍔 Grub & Drinks: Snacks, cafes, fast food
  • 🎮 Fun & Tech: Subscriptions, games, hangouts
  • 🚌 Essentials: Transit, phone plans, school gear
⚡ Micro-Challenge: Check your banking app or wallet right now. Write down the last 3 things you bought. Were any of them a surprise?

Waiting Before Buying Anything

An Effective Method to suggest to improve how you spend money Click to Read
⏳ The "Hours Worked" Mindset

If you make $12/hr at a part-time job, a $60 game isn't $60—it's 5 hours of your life. Ask yourself: Is it worth 5 hours of work?

The Cool-Off Wait List:
< $20 Wait 24 Hrs
$20 - $100 Wait 3 Days
$100+ Wait 1 Week
The Cart Hack: Add items to your online cart, but do not check out. Close the tab. If you still remember it in 3 days, reconsider it!
