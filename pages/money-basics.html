Our Vision

We believe everyone deserves a stress-free relationship with money. Our goal is to give you tools and peer tips that make smart financial choices second nature.

Needs Vs Wants

Are you able to separate your desires? Is it a need or a want?

  • –Need: groceries, rent, essential clothing, phone plan.
  • –Want: fast food, designer fits, streaming subs, gaming.
  • –Ask yourself: will my week break down without this item?
  • –Am I buying this out of stress, boredom, or FOMO?
  • –Would I still want this if it wasn't on sale or trending?
  • –Try the 24-hour rule: leave any 'want' over $20 in your cart for 24 hours — if you still care about it tomorrow, consider it.
