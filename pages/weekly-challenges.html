Mindset Tips

What if I miss a day? + –

Keep the task small, focus on progress and restart without guilt if you miss a day.

How small should each challenge feel? + –

Small enough to fit in five minutes between classes or after dinner. If a challenge feels bigger than that, break it into a smaller first step.

Why bother with a streak at all? + –

A streak isn’t the goal, the habit is. Missing one day and coming back the next still builds the same habit as a perfect week.
