Emergency Preparedness

Because “I’ll figure it out” isn’t a plan. Quick guides for the moments that actually matter.

Preparedness checklist

Six things worth sorting out before you need them — not after.

Save campus security & a trusted contact in your phone

Know two exits from your hostel or room

Keep a small cash stash separate from your wallet

Screenshot your ID and insurance card

Know your building’s fire assembly point

Keep a basic first-aid kit within reach

Common student incident scenarios

Fire alarm goes off at 2am

Grab your keys and phone, leave calmly by the nearest exit, and head straight to the assembly point.

A friend faints in class

Help them lie down with legs raised, loosen tight clothing, and call for help immediately.

You lose your wallet while travelling

Cancel your cards right away, report a lost student ID, and file a police report if needed.

Must-have vs. suggested items

Must-have

  • Student ID / NRIC
  • Phone + charger or power bank
  • A small amount of cash
  • Any regular medication

Nice to have

  • Compact first-aid kit
  • Spare hostel or room key
  • Portable umbrella or poncho
  • Small notebook with emergency numbers

Expandable action guides

If there’s a fire + –

Stay low, head to the nearest marked exit, never use lifts, and gather at the assembly point for a headcount.

If someone faints nearby + –

Keep them lying down with legs slightly raised, loosen tight clothing, call for help, and stay with them.

If you lose your wallet + –

Cancel your cards immediately, report a lost student ID to the office, and file a police report if it happened in public.
