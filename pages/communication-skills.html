Communication skills that matter.

Learn how to communicate effectively in different situations. From group projects to personal messages, master the art of clear and respectful communication.

Why Communication Skills Matter

Strong communication skills are essential for success in university, work, and personal relationships. Effective communication helps you express ideas clearly, resolve conflicts respectfully, and build meaningful connections with others. Whether navigating group projects, asking professors for help, or discussing sensitive topics with friends, good communication prevents misunderstandings and strengthens relationships. These skills boost confidence, enhance your ability to advocate for yourself, and create a foundation for professional success. Ultimately, mastering communication empowers you to influence others positively, achieve your goals, and thrive in any social or professional environment.

Useful phrases for better communication

Starting a difficult conversation

  • • "I wanted to talk about something that's been on my mind..."
  • • "Can we discuss this? I'd like to understand your perspective..."
  • • "I need your help with something. Can we talk?"
  • • "I don't think we're on the same page. Let me explain my side..."

Expressing your feelings respectfully

  • • "When you did that, I felt hurt because..."
  • • "I appreciate what you're saying, but I disagree because..."
  • • "I'm feeling overwhelmed and need some support..."
  • • "That comment made me uncomfortable. Can we talk about it?"

Asking for help or clarification

  • • "Could you explain that a different way? I'm not sure I understand..."
  • • "Would you mind if I asked a question?"
  • • "I need your help with this. Do you have time?"
  • • "Could you give me an example? That would help me understand better."

Showing empathy and understanding

  • • "I can see why you'd feel that way..."
  • • "That sounds really frustrating. How can I help?"
  • • "I hear what you're saying, and I want to understand more..."
  • • "Thank you for sharing that with me. I appreciate your honesty."
