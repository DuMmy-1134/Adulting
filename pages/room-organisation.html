Room Organization

Transform your space with simple, step-by-step guides — desk, wardrobe, and everything in between.

Getting started

A few short modules take you from a cluttered room to an organized one — split it into zones, decide what to keep, store what’s left, then keep it that way.

MODULE 01

Organize by Zone

+ – Divide your room into zones and organize each area strategically for maximum efficiency.
  • Split your room into a handful of zones — desk, wardrobe, storage, floor — instead of tackling "the whole room" at once.
  • Finish one zone completely before moving to the next; partial progress everywhere feels like no progress anywhere.
  • Try the interactive zone checklist further down this page to work through all four zones step by step.
MODULE 02

Decluttering Method

+ – Master the assess, decide, and organize framework to remove clutter effectively.
  • Assess: take everything out of the space and look at what you actually own.
  • Decide: sort into keep, donate, and bin — if you haven’t used it in a year, it’s probably not a keep.
  • Organize: only put "keep" items back, each in a spot you’ll actually remember.
MODULE 03

Storage Solutions

+ – Discover smart storage options and labeling systems to maintain organization.
  • Use clear bins or labels so you can find things without opening every box.
  • Keep frequently-used items at eye level or arm’s reach; store rarely-used items higher up or further back.
  • Vertical storage (stacked bins, over-door organizers) makes use of space a flat desk or floor can’t.
MODULE 04

Maintenance & Best Practices

+ – Learn the do's and don'ts to keep your room organized long-term.
  • Do put things back in their spot right after using them — it’s much faster than a big reset later.
  • Do spend 5 minutes tidying before bed so mess doesn’t build up overnight.
  • Don’t keep a "miscellaneous" pile — it always grows into full-blown clutter.
